--------------------------------------------------
CORRUPTION MASK
--------------------------------------------------

M in {0,1}^{N x V}

M[i,v] = 1:
sample i / view v is corrupted.

Target corrupted sample-view ratio:

rho = 0.5

--------------------------------------------------
EVEN V
--------------------------------------------------

For every sample:

row_sum(M[i]) = V/2

Therefore:

V=2 -> 1 corrupted view
V=4 -> 2 corrupted views
V=6 -> 3 corrupted views

--------------------------------------------------
ODD V
--------------------------------------------------

For V=2m+1:

row_sum(M[i]) in {m,m+1}

Total corrupted cells:

floor(N*V/2)

Current even-N datasets therefore achieve exact
50% total corruption.
